How this list is built

  • Authors: everyone with at least one article in JAE, JAR, TAR, CAR or RAST published 2021–2026, from OpenAlex and the journals' own issue lists, 2,974 people.
  • Pages: the personal website listed on the author's ORCID record, and the faculty profile (plus any personal site or CV it links to) found through a curated list of 148 accounting faculty directories. 1,103 of 2,974 authors had at least one page that could be read (37.1%); the rest list no site we could find, or their pages block automated reading.
  • A paper is listed only when the page itself attaches a revise-and-resubmit phrase to one of the six journals (for example, “R&R at The Accounting Review”). The exact wording is shown under each entry.
  • Papers that Crossref or OpenAlex show as already published are removed, and every entry was checked against the live page before it was listed.
